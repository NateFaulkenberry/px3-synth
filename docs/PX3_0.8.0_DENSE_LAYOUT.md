# PX3 0.8.0 dense layout: one modular surface

The owner's brief: one dense, cohesive synthesizer surface with essentially no
wasted space, composed of tightly packed modules, using VCV Rack's dense
modular information architecture as the layout reference (not its look).
Builds on `PX3_0.8.0_UI_FOUNDATION.md` (scene + layout engine),
`PX3_0.8.0_MODULATION_UI.md` (in-window routing) and
`PX3_0.8.0_VISUAL_REDESIGN.md` (tokens, knob family, faceplates). Main's power
button rule (card chrome placed only by `CardHost::powerBounds()`: 20 px, 6 px
inset, centred below the 2 px accent stripe with 4 px clearance, title band
>= `theme::space::minTitleBand` = 30 px) is kept everywhere; the modulators'
source jacks mirror it on the right edge.

## Research notes

References consulted (current material, October 2026):

- VCV Rack Manual, *Module Panel Guide* (vcvrack.com/manual/Panel): panels
  are fixed-height columns in HP multiples; text readable at 100 %; "roughly
  follow the graphical density and text sizes of VCV modules"; enough room
  between controls to grab them. Take-away for PX3: modules are fixed-height
  columns of controls that tile edge to edge, the rack (not the module) owns
  spacing, and density comes from consistent small type plus controls sized by
  importance, not from shrinking everything.
- VCV Rack / Eurorack patching: a jack per source, cables drawn between jacks,
  the colour of a cable identifies it but its two ends are what make it
  readable. Take-away: sources are jacks on the modules themselves (LFO/ENV
  title bands) and in a source column; a connection is drawn as a lead.
- Bitwig *Unified Modulation System* / The Grid (bitwig.com user guide):
  drag a modulator onto any parameter; depth is a ring on the knob and also a
  number in the modulator's list; Grid inputs carry a small amount knob next
  to the jack. Take-away: the route's amount belongs on the route strip
  itself, as a number you can fine-adjust.
- Serum / Serum 2 (Xfer manual, tutorials): drag a source onto a knob, the
  route appears in the matrix; the matrix row shows source, amount,
  destination, curve, uni/bipolar. Take-away: drag-to-knob and the matrix are
  two views of the same routes (PX3 already does both, the matrix now reads
  as a patch bay rather than a list).
- Vital / Phase Plant (product docs, general knowledge): modulators live next
  to the sound sources on the main page; a modulation tab lists every
  connection with a bipolar amount bar. Reason (rack back panel): routing is
  physical cables between labelled jacks, grouped by device. Take-away: put
  the modulators in VOICE, keep the full patch overview on its own page with
  grouped destinations.

## Design decisions

1. **The window is the rack.** No outer padding: the header, macro rail and
   section views run to the window edges. Every module is separated from its
   neighbour by a 1 px seam of the chassis colour (scene `gap: 1`), card
   margins are 0 (UIConfig `cards.*.margin`), and the faceplate's own edge and
   bevel provide the separation, as the hardware panel edge does in a rack.
2. **Square everything that is a container.** `theme::space::panelRadius` and
   `insetRadius` are 0; UIConfig container radii (cards, graphs, top strip,
   signal flow, mixer buttons, bus inserts, macro depth, update notice) are 0;
   literal radii in the LFO/sub/filter graphs, top menu, macro strip, patch
   bar, drag overlay and matrix are gone. Knobs keep their round geometry.
3. **VOICE is the instrument.** Two rows in one page (scene `view.osc`,
   column):
   - `voice.top` (grow 63): SUB | OSC 1 | OSC 2 | OSC 3 | FILTER 1 | FILTER 2
     (+ SERIES/BALANCE row under the filters) | AMP ENV;
   - `voice.mods` (grow 37, min 230 px): LFO 1 | LFO 2 | LFO 3 | ENV 1 |
     ENV 2 | ENV 3 as one packed row of six equal modules.
   The split, order and the row itself are scene properties: move `env.1`
   before `lfo.1`, give `voice.mods` `wrap`, or change the grow ratio in the
   designer, nothing in code assumes six-across.
4. **Compact modulator interiors** (`CompactModuleLayout.h`, used by
   `LfoComponent::layoutCompact` and `EnvelopeComponent::layoutCompact`): fixed
   12 px captions, 20 px boxes, knobs as large as the row allows (26-50 px),
   and the display (wave / envelope graph) takes whatever height is left.
   - LFO: WAVE | ASSIGN | KEY SYNC; CLOCK | DIVISION; RATE | AMOUNT; wave display.
   - ENV: ASSIGN | LOOP | SYNC; envelope display (editable); A D S R; TYPE |
     AMOUNT | KEY.
   - Each module's source jack mirrors the power button at the right end of
     the title band (same rect, mirrored), so every modulator is a drag source.
   The old CardInner layout is still used when a card is not scene-managed.
5. **Oscillator cards absorb empty rows.** The MACROS row collapses when a
   mode has no table/macros and the graph row grows into the space (scene
   `grow` instead of fixed percentages).
6. **MOD is the modulation matrix / patch bay** (`ModRoutingPanel`): see
   *Matrix interaction model* below.
7. **Header**: 38 px rail, no outer margin, logo 96 px, tabs/preset/menu
   unchanged in behaviour, master gain 44 px. Module title bands are the
   30 px power-button band (`theme::space::minTitleBand`) everywhere (matrix modules included, for one
   rhythm across the surface).
8. **FX page**: grid gap 1 px, strip gap 1 px, card margin 0, and a partial
   last row spreads over the full width so the rack never ends in an empty
   slot.
9. **Keyboard and performance section hidden** (see below). A small notice
   plate (`NoticeBanner.h`) at the bottom centre carries the messages the
   keyboard used to show (macro / MIDI assignment prompts, "Please engage an
   oscillator!") while the section is hidden.

## Scene structure (InstrumentScene.json)

```
instrument (column, gap 1, no padding)
  header (row 38 px, gap 1): header.logo | header.menu | header.gain
  controls (row, grow, gap 1)
    macros (58 px)
    views (overlay)
      view.osc  (column, gap 1)            VOICE
        voice.top (row, gap 1)
          primary.osc: osc.sub | osc.1 | osc.2 | osc.3   (cards; controls are nodes)
          primary.filter (column): filter.cards (filter.1 | filter.2) / filter.routing
          primary.amp: amp.envelope
        voice.mods (row, gap 1): lfo.1 | lfo.2 | lfo.3 | env.1 | env.2 | env.3
      view.mod                                MOD = matrix
        mod.routing (column, gap 1)
          mod.routing.title (24 px header)
          mod.routing.body (row, gap 1)
            mod.routing.patch (SOURCES, 196 px) | mod.routing.list (ROUTES, grow) | mod.routing.dest (DESTINATIONS, 27 %, 250-400 px)
      view.fx | view.mix | view.settings
  patchbar   (visible: false)   retired source strip, still built
  keys       (visible: false)   keyboard + wheels, still built
```

Bindings (`PluginEditorConfig.cpp::bindSceneComponents`): `voice.mods` ->
`ModPanel` (now a direct child of the editor, no viewport), `lfo.N`/`env.N` ->
its six cards, `mod.routing.*` -> the matrix's four parts.

## Matrix interaction model

- **Sources** (left module): 11 jacks (LFO 1-3, ENV 1-3, MACRO 1-5) grouped
  under printed sub-headings, each with its name, a colour swatch (secondary
  cue) and a route count. Drag a jack, or click it to *arm* it.
- **Routes** (centre module): one 30 px strip per connection, in a column
  legend (SOURCE / AMOUNT / POLARITY / CURVE / DESTINATION). Each strip is a
  lead: source plug, source name, amount control, polarity, curve, the slot or
  origin tag printed on the lead, destination group + name, destination plug.
  Leads are drawn across the seams from the source jack to the strip and
  from the strip to the destination row (when that row is on screen), so the
  page reads source -> route -> destination. The rest of the column shows
  empty slots (dashed leads, unlit plugs).
- **Amount** (`AmountControl`): centre-zero bar in the source colour with the
  value printed as "± 35" (bipolar), "∓ 35" (bipolar inverted) or "+12" /
  "−12" (unipolar), with a decimal when fine-tuned ("± 3.5"). Drag = coarse,
  Cmd/Alt-drag = fine, wheel / arrow keys = 1 % (Shift = 0.1 %), the ‹ › ends
  nudge 1 % (with a modifier 0.1 %), double-click or Return = type a number.
  Every change is one host gesture on `mod.routes.slotNN.depth` (card and
  macro routes edit their own amount).
- **Destinations** (right module): every parameter for which the processor's
  `isGraphDestination` is true, read from the parameter catalog (228 today -
  osc, sub, filter, envelopes, LFOs, FX, mixer, spread...), grouped by the
  catalog's group path, foldable, searchable (tokens over group, name and id).
  Each row is a drop target (`DestinationProvider`), shows a dot per source
  patched to it, and lights when its route is selected. Clicking a row with a
  source armed patches it (click-to-patch); otherwise it selects that row's
  first route.
- **Knobs everywhere** are still drop targets (the drag walks the editor);
  dropping on a knob or on a destination row creates the same route.
- Select a strip (or click a destination) to highlight; Delete/Backspace
  removes it; × on the strip removes it; Escape disarms.

## Keyboard / performance section

PX3 Synth retains the keyboard and performance-section implementation, but
this section is currently disabled/hidden from the release UI while the
single-panel instrument architecture is being refined. It may be reintroduced
later or removed entirely if the product direction determines that it is no
longer necessary.

Mechanism: the scene node `keys` (and the retired `patchbar`) carry
`"visible": false`. `PX3SynthAudioProcessorEditor::isPerformanceSectionShown()`
reads the node; `applySceneLayout()` shows/hides `PianoKeyboard`,
`PerformanceControls` and the spark overlay from it, and a hidden node
reserves no space (the views expand to the bottom edge). Flipping the flag in
the designer (or the JSON) brings the section back unchanged; the tests that
cover its implementation do exactly that (`showPerformanceSection`).

## Performance

`PX3Bench ui`, offscreen paint at 1518x918, RelWithDebInfo, same build
config for both binaries (`benchbefore/PX3Bench` = 014b684 baseline), two
interleaved runs each:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| full editor repaint (mean) | 3.16 / 3.17 ms | 1.88 / 1.95 ms |
| timer + idle animation repaint (median) | 8.86 / 9.39 ms | 8.47 / 8.40 ms |
| timer + live note + automation (median) | 10.77 / 11.03 ms | 10.09 / 9.71 ms |

The first dense build was much slower (9.1 ms full paint, 13-14 ms ticks)
because VOICE now shows six more modules. Fixed without touching the audio
thread:
- the six modulator cards are cached images (`setBufferedToImage`), like the
  OSC / FILTER / AMP panels;
- an LFO's animation repaints only its wave display, not the card;
- `EnvelopeComponent::setEnvelopeMode` (called every tick) no longer
  re-lays-out and repaints the card when the mode has not changed - it was
  ~4 ms per tick for three envelope cards;
- `BreakpointEnvelopeEditor::setEnvelope` repaints only on a shape change;
- the 30 Hz knob walk caches parameter lookups (the processor's lookup is a
  linear scan) and knob registration (was an O(n^2) search).

No layout work per frame: `Dense_TimerTickDoesNoLayoutWork` asserts 0
resolves over 30 ticks.

## Remaining issues

- LFO/ENV interiors are laid out by `CompactModuleLayout` (flex-style rows in
  code), not as individual scene nodes; the cards themselves are scene nodes
  and selectable/movable in the designer. Same as before for the filter and
  AMP card interiors.
- At 1100x700 the LFO ASSIGN box truncates "None" and the filter KEY TRK
  knobs are small; at 2400x1400 knobs are capped and the displays take the
  extra room.
- The source column of the matrix has spare height at large sizes; a live
  activity meter per source would use it well (needs a UI-side read of the
  modulators' current values).
- The route strip's × is low-contrast.
- Strips cannot yet be re-targeted by dragging their destination plug
  (re-patching = delete + drop). Escape does not cancel a drag in progress.
- The notice banner overlays the bottom of the modulator row while a
  message is up (by design it is transient; "engage an oscillator" is
  standing while every source is bypassed).
- Not tried interactively (no GUI session): drag feel, wheel/arrow fine
  steps in a real host, the designer UI itself.
