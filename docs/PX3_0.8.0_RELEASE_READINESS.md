## Done since the overnight report

**Release blockers from the overnight report, all resolved**
- **Master overshoot.** The reverb's level matching ran after the output ceiling. It now runs inside the reverb stage, as a per-sample ramp. regress reports 0 failures, and CI gates it.
- **DRIVE aliasing.** DRIVE now uses a polyphase IIR oversampler, bit-identical to JUCE's best quality: 8× at 48 kHz, 4× at 96 kHz. It runs ADAA in double precision and phase-aligns the dry signal. Worst case is −66 dB, previously −23 dB.
- **Envelope modulation depended on the host buffer size.** In-voice targets are now refreshed every 32 samples.

**Bugs**
- A preset was marked edited (*) as soon as it loaded.
- The editor rewrote AMP ENV's enable every tick, fighting host automation.
- A bypassed COMP's needle drew as overlapping translucent parts.
- The save prompt went under the preset sheet after one tick.
- Knob readouts showed 7 decimals.

**Performance**
- Sheets now blur their backdrop once, with a real separable blur (it was 48 resampled full-window draws per paint).
- Animated displays (OSC, SUB, LFO) are opaque layers.
- JUCE now paints each dirty rectangle separately instead of their bounding box.
- The image caches that animations were thrashing are gone.
- DRIVE's ADAA reuses the previous antiderivative.

**Features you asked for**
- Unsaved-changes prompt: SAVE / DON'T SAVE / CANCEL.
- EQ and COMP sheets are draggable.
- COMP art pass.
- Keyboard-case ends on both sides; key sparks and wiggle replaced by a pressed-in, lit key.
- Wheel sparkles removed.
- MIX/LINK centred.
- 1 px seams everywhere.
- INIT: both filters AllPass, DRIVE bypassed.
